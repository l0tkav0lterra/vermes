# Vermes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy describes how Lotka Volterra, publishing under the developer alias Volterra ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the Microsoft Store Windows edition of Vermes and its multiplayer relay service.

**Privacy contact: volterra@atomicmail.io**

## Information stored on your device

Vermes stores your chosen player name, team and character names, game preferences, display and audio settings, controls, and connection preferences on your device. On Windows, the game normally stores these files under `%LOCALAPPDATA%/Vermes/Saved`. The game may also create local diagnostic or crash files when an error occurs.

These local files support gameplay, remember your choices, and help diagnose problems. Saving them does not by itself upload them to us. Player names and other match information are transmitted when you use multiplayer, as described below.

## Online and LAN multiplayer

When you browse, host, or join an online game, your device connects to a multiplayer relay server. The service handles your network address, connection identifiers, player name, lobby name and join code, player roster, readiness, selected map and match settings, game/version compatibility information, and packets needed to run and synchronise the match.

We use this information to list available games, connect players, relay gameplay, maintain connections, and diagnose errors or misuse. Your network address is needed to establish the connection; it is not a precise-location feature.

Other players can see names and match information shared by the game. Public lobby details, including lobby names and player names, can be visible to people browsing available games. For LAN play, the game exchanges discovery, lobby, and gameplay information with devices on your local network and the match host.

Choose a nickname instead of your real name. Do not put contact details or other sensitive personal information in player, team, character, or lobby names. Multiplayer information should not be treated as a private communication channel.

## Server logs and retention

The relay creates operational logs containing timestamps, player names, connection identifiers, lobby creation and joining activity, match start and disconnect events, and connection or routing errors. The server processes gameplay packets to forward them; its current logging does not intentionally archive complete matches or record every gameplay packet.

Active connection and lobby information is held to operate sessions and is removed as connections and rooms close or the server stops. Operational logs persist separately. The current server rotates its log when it exceeds approximately 2 MB and keeps up to three older log files. This is a size-based limit, not a fixed number of days: older entries may remain longer when activity is low. Other information, such as support correspondence, may be retained as necessary to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations.

## Support information

If you contact us, we receive the email address or other contact details you use and anything you voluntarily provide, such as a problem description, screenshots, or diagnostic files. We use it to respond and investigate your request. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

## Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell personal information or use the game to provide targeted advertising. The current Store edition does not include advertising or an in-game purchase system, and it does not require a separate Vermes account or Xbox sign-in.

Information is shared with other players where needed for multiplayer. Network, hosting, and communications providers may process information as necessary to deliver connections and support. We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to investigate abuse or protect people and the service.

Microsoft independently handles Store accounts, purchases, installation, updates, and any diagnostics collected by its platform. Its handling of that information is governed by the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://www.microsoft.com/privacy/privacystatement). Microsoft does not provide us with your account password or payment-card details through Vermes.

If you choose a separately operated relay server or play on a server hosted by another person, that operator controls its own logs and practices. Their handling of information may differ from this policy.

## Your choices and rights

You can play single-player without using online multiplayer. You can change your chosen names and settings, leave a multiplayer session, and remove local saved files from your device. Removing saved files resets the stored preferences and does not remove operational logs already held by a server. Uninstalling the game may leave local saved files behind.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us at the address above to make a request. Include only the information needed to identify the relevant session or support correspondence. Because the game has no separate user account and names are not unique, we may need additional information to locate records and verify a request.

Where applicable, we process multiplayer information to provide the service you request, operational and support information for legitimate interests in maintaining the service and responding to problems, and information required to meet legal obligations. Server and service-provider locations may differ from your location, so multiplayer information may cross national borders.

## Children

Vermes does not request a date of birth or require players to provide their real name. Parents and guardians should help children choose non-identifying nicknames and decide whether to use multiplayer. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, contact us. Store age ratings describe game content and do not remove the privacy considerations of playing with other people.

## Security and changes

No networked service can guarantee complete security. Avoid including sensitive information in multiplayer names or support files. We may update this policy when the game, service, or information-handling practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
