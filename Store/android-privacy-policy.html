Vermes Android Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy describes information handled by the Android editions of Vermes, published by Lotka Volterra, and the multiplayer relay service. Privacy contact: volterra@atomicmail.io.

Information on your device

Vermes saves player, team and character names, game preferences, controls and display/audio settings on your device. It may also create diagnostic or crash logs. These settings are not automatically uploaded to us merely by saving them.

Multiplayer

Online multiplayer sends your network address, chosen names, lobby and join-code information, readiness, map and match settings, game version, and gameplay packets to the host and relay service. Other players can see names and public lobby details. LAN multiplayer exchanges discovery and match information on your local network. Use a nickname and avoid personal information in game names.

The relay keeps operational connection and session logs to run the service and investigate faults or misuse. The current server rotates its operational log at approximately 2 MB and keeps up to three older files. Retention therefore depends on activity rather than a fixed number of days. The protected server dashboard also records current and historic session information. We do not intentionally archive every gameplay packet as a complete match recording.

Advertising in the Google Play edition

The Google Play edition uses Google AdMob banner ads on loading screens, the main menu and match results. Ads do not appear during active gameplay. The separate direct-download APK uses Google's sample test ads.

Google's advertising SDK may process network addresses, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information, and approximate location inferred from network information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve its services. The game does not request precise-location permission for advertising. How data is used depends on your region, device settings and privacy choices.

The Google User Messaging Platform requests consent where required. Real ad requests wait until that platform indicates they are permitted. You can manage or withdraw applicable choices through Settings > Display > Ad Privacy Choices when that option is required for your region. Declining consent does not prevent playing the game. Google may serve limited or non-personalised ads where permitted.

Google describes its practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can also manage advertising privacy controls in your Android settings.

Store services and updates

Google independently handles Play accounts, downloads, updates, purchases and platform diagnostics under its policies. The Play edition can check for updates through Google Play. The separate direct-download APK can check GitHub for newer releases. These connections expose ordinary network information to the relevant service. Vermes does not receive your Google password or payment-card details.

Support and sharing

If you contact us, we receive the contact details and information you choose to provide and use them to respond and investigate. Multiplayer information is shared with players and service providers as necessary to operate the service. Information may also be disclosed where legally required or to investigate abuse. Hosting and service providers may process information in countries outside your own. Separately operated game hosts and relay servers have their own practices.

Choices and privacy requests

You can play single-player or local hot-seat without online multiplayer, leave multiplayer sessions, change names and settings, and clear the app's local storage. Clearing local data does not erase existing server records. Advertising and update checks may still use the network when available.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to processing, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact volterra@atomicmail.io with enough information to identify the relevant session or request. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. Player names are not unique, so additional information may be needed to locate a record.

Children and changes

The game does not ask players for their real name or date of birth. Parents and guardians should help children choose non-identifying nicknames and decide whether to use online features. Contact us if you believe a child has shared personal information that should be removed. We may update this policy as the game or its services change; the revision date appears above.
